# Mission 3: Validate, launch, and grow responsibly

Read `AGENTS.md`, `docs/PRD.md`, `research/PRODUCT_THESIS.md`, `research/COMPETITOR_MATRIX.md`, and `research/QA_LOG.md`.

Do not assume the extension is wanted just because it works. Create a 30-day validation and distribution plan based on the chosen beachhead audience:
- Current Chrome Web Store discoverability and naming for both “NotebookLM” and “Gemini Notebook” without keyword stuffing or implying Google affiliation.
- A product page/listing that explains one job, shows the real workflow in a short demo, includes honest screenshots, clear permission/privacy language, troubleshooting, and a feedback channel.
- Community-specific distribution opportunities with each community's rules. No spam, fake reviews, paid undisclosed endorsements, or fabricated testimonials.
- A first-user testing plan: recruit relevant users, observe unprompted use, log failure modes, ask what they used before, and test willingness to pay only after successful repeated value.
- Metrics: listing impression-to-install if available, activation (first successful export), export success rate, time-to-first-value, repeat use, support burden, uninstall/negative review reasons, and paid conversion only if a paid feature exists.
- A weekly experiment backlog ranked by expected learning, impact, cost, and confidence. Include explicit kill/pivot criteria.

Have an independent growth critic challenge the plan for unsupported assumptions and distribution bottlenecks. Do not purchase ads or publish/release anything without approval. Present a practical plan with sources and clearly label unknowns.
